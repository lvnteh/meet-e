What if your meeting could cancel itself ? 🎉

The Problem

Some meetings are just reading sessions. Inefficient. You spend 40 minutes catching up on what a document could have covered in five.

The Solution

Meet-e moves your meeting into done. Before it happened. Participants read, engage, and approve async. AI keeps everyone moving. If the team agrees before the deadline — the meeting cancels itself. No rescheduling. No follow-up. Done.

🧠
The Context Layer

Every skipped meeting builds your AI knowledge base.

Decisions, comments, and sign-offs are captured in structured, searchable documents. Your AI agents run on context — this is how you build it. Every meeting you don't hold still makes your team smarter.
