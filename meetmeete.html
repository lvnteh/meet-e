The Problem

Most meetings exist to align people on a document or decision. But participants arrive unprepared, the first hour is spent reading what should have been pre-read, and a follow-up meeting gets scheduled to finish the conversation.

The Solution

Meet-e makes async work explicit and AI-supervised. The meeting becomes a deadline. Participants read, comment, and approve on their own schedule. If everyone agrees before the deadline — the meeting cancels itself.

📅Deadline

AI agent monitors progress hourly — nudges the quiet ones, generates the summary, cancels the meeting if everyone's aligned.

Structured by design

Organizer picks a document template (decision record, kickoff, retro). Phases and timing are defined upfront in a simple YAML config.

🤖

AI does the herding

Claude Code agent runs hourly. It posts phase announcements, nudges silent reviewers, generates the final summary, and updates the KPI tracker.

📊

Tracks hours saved

Every cancelled meeting adds to a running total in the GitHub README. Concrete ROI, visible to the whole team.
